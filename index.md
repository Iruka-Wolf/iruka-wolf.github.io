# About Me

I’m a furry from the midwest with a passion for video games and music! I make original instrumentals and occasionally do video game music covers.

I tend to write in two distinct styles: **orchestral** and **instrumental rock**.

80's and 2000's Rock is my main inspiration for the latter, so expect a plethora of power chords and even more powerful drums!

As for the former, I draw inspiration from many games from my childhood and games I'm continuing to discover today. Composers like Koji Kondo, Motoi Sakuraba, and Nobuo Uematsu are my greatest role-models.

# Follow my personal music endeavors on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@iruka_wolf)!

Most of my tracks are pushed to YouTube exclusively on a monthly upload schedule, with yearly releases going through DistroKid. This is to maintain cost-effectiveness and help build an audience. Many of my compositions never see the light of day, but at least once a month I'll release a track on YouTube.
